why m before bed?

Because nighttime has a way of making everything a little quieter—enough to finally hear what we’ve been thinking all day.

M Before Bed is a personal diary, a collection of thoughts, and a small safe-feeling space on the internet.

I don’t write about one thing. I write about life as it happens: what I’m learning, what I’m questioning, what hurts, what heals, what makes me laugh, and whatever else feels worth leaving on the page.

I hope you can come here at the end of the day and find something that feels human, comforting, or simply familiar.

Stay for a little while.

— M.
